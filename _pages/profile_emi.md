Emi Soroka is a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering. Her current work focuses on developing mathematically grounded approaches to improving the performance of large language models, although she is interested in foundation models of all types. Emi is interested in optimization, statistics and large language models and is the main developer of [Satisfiability.jl](https://github.com/elsoroka/Satisfiability.jl): a Julia package for interacting with SMT solvers. She is an active member of the graduate community and volunteers with the Stanford Graduate Workers Union as a data scientist. When not conducting research, she enjoys climbing and hanging out with friends.
